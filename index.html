Your Accessibility Checklist

Before you deploy anything — run through these seven points. It takes five minutes and catches the most frequent barriers.

  • Images & Text Every image has a descriptive alt text — or an empty alt="" if decorative
  • Images & Text Every button has a descriptive label — not just "click here" or an icon only
  • Images & Text Every form field has a visible label — not just placeholder text
  • Visual Colour contrast meets WCAG Level AA — 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text
  • Visual No information is conveyed by colour alone — add a label, icon, or pattern
  • Structure & Navigation Every interactive element is reachable by keyboard — Tab through your page to check
  • Structure & Navigation Heading structure is logical — H1 → H2 → H3, no levels skipped
